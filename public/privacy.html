Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Last updated October 30, 2026Texas WebCoders

This Privacy Policy explains how Texas WebCoders (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal information when you visit texaswebcoders.com, call or text us, talk to our AI voice assistant, or hire us. We don’t sell your personal information.

1. Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your name, business name, email address and phone number, when you contact us, request a quote or book a consultation. Messages you send through our website forms and chatbot are delivered to us by email through our email and form delivery providers.
  • Project information you share with us, such as content, files, logins and business details.
  • Payment information, which is entered directly with our payment processor (such as Stripe). We don’t see or store your full card number.

2. Calls with our AI voice assistant

When you tap “Talk to our AI” on our website or speak with Sarah, our AI assistant, by phone, your voice is processed by our voice AI provider so the assistant can understand and answer you. These conversations may be recorded and transcribed, and we use the transcripts to follow up with you, book appointments and improve the assistant. Your browser will ask for permission before using your microphone. If you’d rather not talk to an AI assistant, call us at (903) 222-6022 during business hours.

Website chatbot

Our website chatbot is an AI assistant. What you type may be processed by our AI provider to generate replies, and chats during a booking are sent to our team with your request. Please don't share sensitive information such as card numbers or passwords in the chat.

3. Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your phone number and agree to receive text messages, we may text you about your inquiry, appointments and projects. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share or sell your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and pages viewed, to deliver and secure the site. Our pages also load content from third-party services, such as fonts, code libraries, embedded Google Maps and portfolio media, and those providers may receive your IP address when your browser requests them. We don’t use advertising cookies. Your browser may store small preferences locally to make the site work.

5. How we use information

  • To answer your questions, prepare quotes and deliver our services.
  • To schedule consultations and send updates about your project.
  • To process payments and keep business records.
  • To improve our website, services and AI assistant.
  • To protect our website and comply with the law.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, voice AI and telephony, payment processing, and scheduling tools. They may use it only to provide their services to us. We may also share information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a business transfer. We don’t sell personal information.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide our services and for legitimate business and legal purposes, such as tax records, and then delete or anonymize it.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing messages. Depending on where you live, including under Texas law, you may have additional privacy rights. To make a request, email info@texaswebcoders.com. We’ll verify your request and respond within the time required by law.

10. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version.
