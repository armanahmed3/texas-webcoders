What does an AI calling agent do?

An AI calling agent is a voice AI that holds real phone conversations. It answers calls and follows up with leads, using a script we build with you, so calls stop going to voicemail and your team stops doing it all by hand.

01

Inbound call answering

Answers every call 24/7 and handles the common questions.

02

Lead follow-up calls

Calls back leads who asked to be contacted.

03

Lead qualification

Asks the questions you approve and notes the answers.

04

Appointment booking

Books into Google or Outlook Calendar on the Business plan.

05

Urgent call transfer

Sends urgent calls straight to your team.

06

Managed for you

We build, test and tune the agent, so you don’t need a tech team.

How we build your AI calling agent

  1. Tell us about your calls and leads at a free consultation.
  2. We write the call script and train the agent on your business.
  3. You call it, test it and we refine how it speaks.
  4. It goes live, most often in 7–14 days, and we keep tuning it.
