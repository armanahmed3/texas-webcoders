What Texas businesses should know before choosing an AI receptionist

English and Spanish on every call

Spanish is spoken at home by a large share of Texans, and in many Rio Grande Valley and border cities it is the first language for most callers. Our AI receptionists answer in English and Spanish, so callers can book, ask questions and leave details in the language they prefer. Our El Paso and Mission guides show what this looks like locally.

Time zones and hours

Most of Texas runs on Central Time, but El Paso and Hudspeth counties use Mountain Time. We set your hours, booking times and after-hours rules to the clock your customers use.

Recording calls in Texas

Texas is generally treated as a one-party consent state for recording calls, but callers from other states may be covered by stricter rules. If you record calls, tell callers at the start and check with your advisor.

Industries we set up most often in Texas

  • HVAC and home services: summer heat and storms bring urgent calls while crews are on jobs.
  • Roofing: hail days can bring dozens of calls in one afternoon.
  • Medical and dental offices: patients book and reschedule at any hour.
  • Law firms: new clients reach intake before they call the next firm.
  • Restaurants: reservations and hours questions during service.

What setup looks like

Everything is handled remotely. We write your script with you, connect your calendar and CRM, test real calls with you, then forward your number. Most Texas businesses go live in 7 to 14 days, and plans start at $149/month plus a $299 one-time setup.
