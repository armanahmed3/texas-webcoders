What can an AI receptionist do for HVAC and contractors?

When you’re on a roof or under a house, you can’t answer the phone. An AI receptionist catches those calls, so a homeowner with no AC doesn’t move on to the next company.

01

Answer on every job

Picks up while your whole crew is busy, day or night.

02

Capture job details

Gets the address, the problem and the best contact number.

03

Book service visits

Offers open slots and books into Google or Outlook Calendar.

04

Flag emergencies

Follows your rules for no heat, no AC or leaks, and transfers urgent calls.

05

Quote common services

Shares the prices or service fees you approve.

06

Text you each lead

Every call arrives as a short summary on your phone.

How a service call works

  1. A homeowner calls at 11 PM because the AC stopped working.
  2. The AI gets the address and the problem, then checks if it’s urgent.
  3. It books a morning visit, or transfers the call under your emergency rules.
  4. You get a text with the job details before your first call of the day.
