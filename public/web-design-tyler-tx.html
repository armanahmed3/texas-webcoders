Tyler, Texas

Website design for Tyler businesses

Tyler is the county seat of Smith County and a center for health care, education and shopping in East Texas, so many Tyler websites need to reach customers in surrounding towns as well as in the city. Education and health care employ about 30% of Tyler workers and retail about 12%, according to the U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2024 5-year estimates.

What Tyler businesses usually need from a website:

  • Clinics and dental offices: clear service pages and online booking.
  • Contractors and home services: a list of the towns you truly serve, such as Whitehouse, Bullard or Lindale, and a fast quote form.
  • Shops and restaurants: hours, menus, directions and Google Maps details that are always up to date.
  • Spanish speakers: about 18.4% of Tyler residents speak Spanish at home, so a Spanish page or two can help.

Every website includes a mobile-first design, SSL security and contact forms, and our Starter plan includes local SEO and Google Maps setup.
