Quick answers

AI receptionists and AI agents, explained simply

Short, clear answers to the questions Texas business owners ask us most about AI receptionists and automation.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is a voice agent that answers your business phone 24/7. It talks with callers in a natural voice and answers common questions. It books appointments into your calendar and texts you a summary of every call. It works in English or Spanish, so no caller goes to voicemail.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

AI receptionist plans start at $149 a month plus a $299 one-time setup. That covers up to 250 call minutes. The Business plan is $299 a month plus $499 setup. It adds calendar booking, lead screening, urgent call transfer and a link to your CRM.

How long does it take to set up?

Most AI receptionists and chatbots go live in 7 to 14 days. That time covers writing your script, training the agent on your business, connecting your calendar and CRM, and testing real calls with you before your number is forwarded.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

That is your choice. We can set the greeting to say it is an AI assistant, and the voice sounds natural either way. Callers can always ask to reach a person, and urgent calls can be transferred straight to your team or your on-call phone.

Which businesses use AI receptionists?

They help most where calls are frequent and a missed call means a lost customer: clinics and dental offices, law firms, HVAC and home services, roofers, real estate agents, insurance agents and restaurants. Busy owners use them to cover nights, weekends and the moments when every line is taken.

Where is Texas WebCoders located?

Texas WebCoders is based at 5221 S Broadway Ave in Tyler, Texas. Our team works Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm. We build AI receptionists, chatbots and websites for businesses across Texas and the rest of the United States, and most projects run fully online, so you never need to visit.

What is an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot is a chat assistant that answers customer questions in writing, day and night. Ours work on your website, WhatsApp, text and Instagram. They learn your services, prices and policies, capture contact details from every lead and book appointments straight into your calendar.

What is AI automation?

AI automation means letting AI agents do routine office work for you. They move data between your apps, fill in forms, handle invoices and send follow-ups on time. Your team spends less time on copy and paste and more time with customers. We connect it to the tools you already use.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short call with us. We ask about the calls you get, your services and prices, and the tools you use, like your calendar or CRM. Then we write the script, load your FAQs and prices and do the setup for you. You test everything before launch.

What is included in a monthly AI plan?

Monthly AI plans include hosting, updates and ongoing tuning of your agent. We check call notes for mistakes and improve the answers each month. One-time builds, such as apps, automations and websites, come with full source code that you own. Need something new? Just ask for a quote.

How to set up an AI receptionist

Setting up an AI receptionist takes five clear steps, so it gets things right. We map out the calls you get, connect your booking tools and test every answer before your phone line goes live.

  1. List your common calls and what should happen next.
  2. Set the rules for bookings, messages and transfers.
  3. Connect your tools, such as your calendar and CRM.
  4. Test real calls, including hard questions.
  5. Forward your number for all calls, after hours or missed calls.

How to choose an AI receptionist provider

To pick the best AI receptionist, look at three things: how the voice sounds, which tools it works with and what it costs each month. Call the demo lines. Check that it speaks good English and Spanish. Make sure urgent calls can go straight to your on-call team.

  1. Call each demo line and listen to the voice.
  2. Test it in English and Spanish if your callers use both.
  3. Ask who writes the script and how changes are made later.
  4. Compare the full monthly cost, including setup and extra minutes.
  5. Check how urgent calls reach a real person.
