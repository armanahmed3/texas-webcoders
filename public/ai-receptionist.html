How it works

From ring to booked in one call.

Your AI receptionist follows the same steps your best front-desk person would, every single time.

01 · ANSWER

Picks up on the first ring

Greets callers by your business name in a warm, natural voice, day or night.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Listens and answers

Handles questions about services, hours, pricing and location from your own script.

03 · ACT

Books, routes or takes a message

Checks your calendar, books the slot, or transfers urgent calls to your team.

04 · REPORT

Sends you the summary

A text and email with caller details, the transcript and what happens next.
