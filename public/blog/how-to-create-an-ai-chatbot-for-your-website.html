How to Create an AI Chatbot for Your Website

A step-by-step guide to creating an AI chatbot for your website: goals, training, tools, testing, costs, and when to build it yourself or hire a team.

Nov 3, 20267 min readBy the Texas WebCoders team

To create an AI chatbot for your website, decide what it should do, train it on your business information, connect it to your lead or booking tools, test it with real questions and then add it to your site. Simple chatbots can be built with an online builder in a day. A custom AI chatbot usually takes 7 to 14 days.

Step 1: Decide what the chatbot should do

Pick one or two jobs first. Common goals are answering frequent questions, capturing leads, booking appointments and checking order status. A chatbot that tries to do everything at once is harder to get right.

Step 2: Gather your business information

An AI chatbot is only as good as what it knows. Collect your services, prices, hours, service areas, policies and the questions customers ask most. Your website and FAQ pages are a good start.

Step 3: Choose how to build it

  • Online chatbot builders: fast and low cost for simple question-and-answer bots.
  • Platform add-ons: many website and e-commerce platforms offer chat plugins.
  • Custom AI chatbot development: trained on your business, connected to your CRM and calendar, and tested before launch.

Step 4: Connect your tools

Decide where new leads go, such as your email or CRM, and whether the chatbot can book visits in your calendar. Also decide when it should hand a conversation to a real person.

Step 5: Write the rules

Tell the chatbot what it must never do, such as promise a price it does not know or give legal or medical advice. Set the tone of voice so it sounds like your business.

Step 6: Test it with hard questions

Ask awkward and unclear questions, not only easy ones. Check prices, hours and service areas. Fix every wrong or vague answer before launch.

Step 7: Add it to your website and improve it

Most chatbots are added with a small code snippet. After launch, read real conversations every week and update the answers.

Should you build it yourself or hire a team?

Build it yourself if you need simple answers and have time to maintain it. Hire a team if the chatbot needs to capture leads into your CRM, book appointments, work across several channels or reflect detailed business rules. To compare a chatbot with human chat, read AI chatbot vs live chat.

Common questions

Can I create an AI chatbot for free?

Some builders offer free plans with limits on messages or features. They can work for simple questions, but lead capture, CRM connections and custom training usually need a paid plan or custom development.

How long does it take to build an AI chatbot?

A simple bot can be set up in a day. A custom AI chatbot trained on your business usually goes live in 7 to 14 days, including testing.

Do I need coding skills to build a chatbot?

Not for a basic builder. Connecting a chatbot to your CRM, calendar and website rules usually needs some technical setup, which a development team can handle for you.
