AI Receptionist Buyer's Guide: Cost, Features and How to Choose

What an AI receptionist does, what it costs, which features matter and how to compare AI receptionist providers, reviews, free trials and data privacy.

Nov 3, 20269 min readBy the Texas WebCoders team

An AI receptionist is a voice agent that answers your business phone 24/7, answers questions, books appointments and sends you a summary of each call. Prices vary by provider and usage. Texas WebCoders plans start at $149/month plus a $299 one-time setup. The right choice depends on voice quality, booking and CRM connections, language support, data privacy and the help you get with setup.

What does an AI receptionist do?

The primary functions are the same across most providers:

  • Answer every call, day and night
  • Answer common questions about hours, services and prices
  • Book, change and cancel appointments
  • Qualify new leads and send them to your CRM
  • Transfer urgent calls to a person
  • Text or email you a summary of each call

Which features should you look for?

  1. Natural voice: call the demo line yourself and listen.
  2. Booking: check that it works with your calendar, such as Google Calendar, Outlook or Calendly.
  3. CRM integration: new leads should land in your CRM, such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or GoHighLevel.
  4. Phone system: confirm you can keep your number and forward calls for all hours, after hours or only when no one answers.
  5. Languages: if your callers speak Spanish, test the Spanish voice too.
  6. Urgent call rules: you should decide which calls go straight to a person.
  7. Setup help: ask who writes the script and how changes are made later.

How to compare AI receptionist providers

There are three common types: self-service AI receptionist platforms, traditional answering services that have added AI, and agencies that build and manage a custom AI receptionist for you. Self-service tools can be cheaper but leave the setup to you. Managed services cost more to set up but do the script, testing and changes for you. Ask every provider the same questions about price, minutes, setup, integrations and support, then call each demo line.

Where to find honest reviews

Check software review sites such as G2, Capterra and Trustpilot, the provider's Google reviews and agency directories such as Clutch. Look for reviews from businesses like yours, and ask the provider for a reference you can call.

Free trials and demos

Some platforms offer free trials. Others, including Texas WebCoders, offer a free demo and consultation instead. You can hear our live voice agent on our AI receptionist page and book a free demo.

Data privacy and compliance

Ask how call recordings and transcripts are stored, who can access them and how long they are kept. Health care practices should ask whether the provider will sign a business associate agreement (BAA) for HIPAA. Call recording laws differ by state, so tell callers when calls are recorded and check with your advisor. At Texas WebCoders, HIPAA-aware workflows for clinics are part of the Enterprise plan.

Which industries benefit most?

AI receptionists help most where calls are frequent and missed calls cost money: medical offices, dental offices, law firms, HVAC and home services, real estate, restaurants and insurance agents. Startups and small teams also benefit, because an AI receptionist covers the phone without a full-time hire.

Common questions

What is the main job of an AI receptionist?

To answer every call, help the caller with common requests such as questions and bookings, and pass on the details or the call itself to your team.

Can an AI receptionist work with my existing phone system?

Usually, yes. Most services work by forwarding your existing number, either for all calls, after hours or only when no one answers.

Do AI receptionists support other languages?

Many support several languages. Texas WebCoders AI receptionists speak English and Spanish.

Is an AI receptionist good for appointment scheduling?

Yes. Scheduling is one of the most common uses. Check that it connects to your calendar and follows your booking rules.
