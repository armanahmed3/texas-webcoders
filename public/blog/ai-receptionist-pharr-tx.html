Why Pharr businesses miss calls

Pharr’s international bridge is a major crossing for fresh produce from Mexico, which supports cold storage, trucking and customs-related work around the city. Over 80% of residents speak Spanish at home, among the highest shares of the Texas cities we cover. Clinics, schools and family businesses handle many Spanish calls every day. A truck yard, a broker and a dental office here can all lose business from one unanswered phone.

Can you keep your Pharr phone number?

Yes. Pharr callers will usually see the 956 area code, and you can keep the number customers already know. Forward it to the AI for all calls, only after hours, or only when your team does not pick up. We can also set up a new local number. Everything is set up remotely, so you do not need new equipment or a visit to an office.

What to check before you go live

  1. Test real calls: ask awkward questions, not only easy ones, and listen to how it handles them.
  2. Set the rules: decide which calls transfer to a person and which become messages.
  3. Plan your minutes: estimate your busiest month, not an average one.
  4. Tell callers what you need to: Texas is generally treated as a one-party consent state for recording calls, but rules differ for callers in other states, so check with your advisor if you record calls.
